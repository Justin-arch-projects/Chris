The clocks

Total lead time

T0 → T13

Dropped off or picked up, to back with the customer.

Shop lead time

T0 → T11

In the door to every rug and pad on the order done. This is the one to hold to the 10-business-day target.

Step cycle times

start → finish at each gate

Hands-on time per step, plus soak and dry time, which are process time rather than labor.

Wait times

finish → next start

Time in the staging areas: beside the duster, in the wash and tank areas, in the finishing line, in repair.

Notify lag

T11 → T12

Work complete to the customer being called.

Return lag

T12 → T13

Customer called to rug picked up or delivered. Mostly the customer's timing, so tracked apart from the shop.

What the tracker has to handle

  1. Work orders with several rugs. Each rug moves on its own, but the order only completes when all of them are done.
  2. Pads as their own track. Measured at dry-room checkout, cut in parallel, and also started from a customer's call for a pad only.
  3. Repair and finishing in either order. Repair can send a rug on to finishing or straight to done; repairs can go in-house or to an outside vendor.
  4. Timers inside a step. Total finishing time, sprout and loose-yarn trimming time, and dye-bleed treatment time per rug.
  5. The completion rule. No call to the customer until every rug, pad, repair and pet treatment on the order is marked done.
  6. Three closing milestones. Work complete, customer notified, and picked up or delivered, each with its own time.

Still to confirm

  1. Do standard-wash rugs go to the same wash floor and get the same wash and flush as decontaminated rugs? The map assumes yes.
  2. What does "tag to be cut" on the rug tag mean: removing a manufacturer's label, or something else?
  3. Who works each station, and do people move between stations during the day?
  4. Can a rug go from finishing back to repair, for example when dye bleed shows up during finishing?
  5. For vendor repairs, should the tracker record when the rug leaves and comes back from the vendor?
  6. For home pickups, does the clock start when the team documents the rugs at the home, or when they arrive at the shop?
